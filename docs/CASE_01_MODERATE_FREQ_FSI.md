# Case File 01: Moderate-Frequency Dynamic Challenges (FSI)
**Focus:** Fluid-Structure Interaction (FSI) under High-Decibel Acoustic Loading and Modal Phase Cancellation

---

## Executive Summary
In high-velocity fluid environments (e.g., turbofan ducting, gas turbine exhaust, supersonic intakes), high-decibel acoustic forcing couples with structural bending modes, generating constructive wave interference and severe high-cycle fatigue (HCF).

This case is resolved using a self-adaptive **Fe-Mn-Si-Cr-Ni-C Shape Memory Alloy (Fe-SMA) Metamaterial Shell**. By dynamically shifting its elastic modulus under operational temperature and stress, the shell shifts structural natural frequencies out of lock-in alignment with acoustic cavity modes.
